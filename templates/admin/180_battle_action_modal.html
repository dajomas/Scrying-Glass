Battle actions

Start of turn damage and healing

Apply these changes to the current combatant before choosing their actions. Leave unused fields empty. Damage is applied before healing.

Add actions and reactions in resolution order (top to bottom). Reactions have their own source and target, including down or dead combatants. Statistics reduction rows resolve in the same top-to-bottom order as damage and healing. Apply leaves this window open; applied rows are locked and skipped on later applies. Next discards pending rows, advances once and opens the new turn owner. It does not Apply or Undo. Close discards only unapplied rows. Use one row per affected creature for death bursts. A dead combatant cannot be revived by healing; recover it explicitly in Encounter Tools.

Restore statistics

Only statistics with active reductions are shown. Restoring removes reductions; it never heals current HP or revives a combatant.
